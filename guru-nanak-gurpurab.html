About Guru Nanak Gurpurab

📖 Guru Nanak Gurpurab 📖

Guru Nanak Gurpurab, also known as Guru Nanak Jayanti, celebrates the birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, the founder of Sikhism and the first of the ten Sikh Gurus.

Why is it celebrated?

The festival honors Guru Nanak's life and teachings, which emphasized equality, honest living, and devotion to one God. It is one of the most sacred days in the Sikh calendar.

How the Day Unfolds
  1. Akhand Path - A continuous, 48-hour reading of the Guru Granth Sahib leading up to the day
  2. Prabhat Pheris - Early morning devotional processions
  3. Nagar Kirtan - A procession through the streets led by the Panj Pyare, with hymn singing
How People Celebrate
  • Visiting gurdwaras for special prayers
  • Taking part in the Nagar Kirtan procession
  • Listening to hymns (kirtan) and readings from the Guru Granth Sahib
  • Volunteering and serving in the community langar (free kitchen)
  • Lighting up homes and gurdwaras
Celebrating a Message of Equality

At its heart, Guru Nanak Gurpurab celebrates a message that has endured for centuries: equality, humility, and selfless service to others.

Happy Gurpurab! 📖
