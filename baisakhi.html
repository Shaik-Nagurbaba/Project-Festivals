About Baisakhi

🌾 Baisakhi 🌾

Baisakhi is a spring harvest festival celebrated mainly in Punjab, India. For Sikhs, it also marks one of the most important days in their history.

Why is it celebrated?

Baisakhi celebrates the wheat harvest, but it holds special significance for Sikhs as the day in 1699 when Guru Gobind Singh founded the Khalsa, a community of initiated Sikhs.

Why Baisakhi Matters
  • Harvest festival - Farmers celebrate the ripening of the rabi (wheat) crop
  • Founding of the Khalsa - Guru Gobind Singh established the Khalsa in 1699
  • Sikh New Year - Many Sikhs also observe Baisakhi as the start of the new year
How People Celebrate
  • Visiting gurdwaras for prayers
  • Taking part in Nagar Kirtan processions
  • Performing the energetic Bhangra and Giddha folk dances
  • Sharing festive meals with family
  • Celebrating the harvest with fairs and markets
A Festival of Harvest and History

At its heart, Baisakhi blends the joy of a bountiful harvest with the pride of a pivotal moment in Sikh history.

Happy Baisakhi! 🌾
