About Maha Shivaratri

🔱 Maha Shivaratri 🔱

Maha Shivaratri, meaning the "Great Night of Shiva," is a Hindu festival dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is observed with fasting, prayer, and an all-night vigil.

Why is it celebrated?

The festival marks the night Lord Shiva is believed to have performed his cosmic dance of creation, preservation, and destruction. Some traditions also celebrate it as the night of Shiva's marriage to Goddess Parvati.

How the Night Is Observed
  1. Fasting - Devotees fast through the day, some without water
  2. Night vigil - Devotees stay awake through the night in prayer
  3. Temple worship - Offerings of milk, water, and bel leaves are made to the Shiva lingam
How People Celebrate
  • Fasting for the day
  • Visiting Shiva temples and offering prayers
  • Chanting the mantra "Om Namah Shivaya"
  • Staying awake through the night (jagran)
  • Offering milk, water, and bel leaves to the Shiva lingam
A Night of Devotion

At its heart, Maha Shivaratri is about spiritual discipline, devotion, and reflecting on the balance between creation and destruction.

Happy Maha Shivaratri! 🔱
