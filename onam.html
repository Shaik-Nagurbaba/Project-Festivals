About Onam

🌼 Onam 🌼

Onam is the biggest harvest festival of Kerala, India, celebrated over ten days. It brings together people of all faiths and communities in the state for a shared celebration.

Why is it celebrated?

Onam commemorates the legendary King Mahabali, whose rule is remembered as a golden age of prosperity and fairness. It is believed that Onam marks his annual visit to see his people.

Onam Highlights
  • Pookalam - Intricate flower carpets laid at the entrance of homes
  • Onasadya - A grand vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf
  • Vallamkali - Traditional snake boat races held on the rivers of Kerala
How People Celebrate
  • Creating pookalam (flower rangoli) at the doorstep
  • Preparing the Onasadya feast with many dishes
  • Watching or taking part in snake boat races
  • Wearing traditional Kerala attire
  • Performing folk dances like Thiruvathira
A Festival for Everyone

At its heart, Onam celebrates unity, prosperity, and the memory of a just and fair ruler, welcomed home each year with joy.

Happy Onam! 🌼
