About Ganesh Chaturthi

🐘 Ganesh Chaturthi 🐘

Ganesh Chaturthi is a Hindu festival celebrating the birth of Lord Ganesha, the elephant-headed god of wisdom, prosperity, and good fortune. It is especially popular in the Indian state of Maharashtra.

Why is it celebrated?

The festival marks the birthday of Lord Ganesha, who is worshipped as the remover of obstacles and is invoked at the start of new ventures. Clay idols of Ganesha are installed in homes and public pandals for worship.

How the Festival Unfolds
  1. Pratishtapana - Clay idols of Ganesha are installed in homes and public pandals
  2. Daily worship - Prayers, songs, and offerings are made for up to 10 days
  3. Visarjan - The idols are carried in a grand procession and immersed in water
How People Celebrate
  • Installing a clay idol of Ganesha at home or in a public pandal
  • Offering modak, a sweet dumpling said to be Ganesha's favorite
  • Singing devotional songs (aartis)
  • Decorating the idol and surroundings with flowers and lights
  • Joining processions for the final immersion (visarjan)
Welcoming Wisdom and Prosperity

At its heart, Ganesh Chaturthi is about inviting wisdom, good fortune, and a fresh start into one's home and community.
